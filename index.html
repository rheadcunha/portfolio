Research and data analysis

Selected analyses from coursework and research, with the headline estimate where there is one.

Mortality analysis, Health and Retirement Study

Education, cognition, and mortality

Among 9,627 HRS participants, higher cognitive scores were associated with lower mortality risk (HR 0.982), with a slightly stronger effect among those with more than 12 years of education.

  • Cox regression
  • Kaplan-Meier
  • Interaction analysis
  • Cognitive aging

Cross-sectional, UNICEF WASH

Socioeconomic status and access to improved water in rural Zambia

Multivariable logistic regression (n = 1,170) found higher-wealth households had greater odds of improved water access (aOR 2.07, 95% CI 1.62–2.64).

  • R
  • Logistic regression
  • UNICEF WASH

Child health, 2018 Zambia DHS

Household wealth and child growth outcomes

Cross-sectional analysis (n = 8,744) using ANOVA, linear and logistic regression linking household wealth to stunting (aOR 1.25, 95% CI 1.08–1.45).

  • SAS
  • ANOVA
  • Multivariable regression

Severe stunting by household wealth
