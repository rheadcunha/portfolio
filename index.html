Public health · DHS 2018

Zambia DHS 2018: Severe Stunting Analysis

Using multiple linear regression (n=8,744), children from the poorest households had lower height-for-age Z-scores than the middle wealth group (β=-0.121), while children from the richest households had higher scores (β=0.169). In multivariable logistic regression, the poorer group had higher odds of severe stunting (OR=1.251), and the richest group had lower odds (OR=0.743).

ANOVA Multiple linear regression Multivariable logistic regression Interaction analysis
